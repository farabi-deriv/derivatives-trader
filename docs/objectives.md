# Derivatives Trader — Objectives

**Status:** not yet authored · **Owner:** `nijil-deriv`

No outcome targets, OKRs, or success measures are recorded anywhere in this
repository, so none are stated here. When they are set, they belong in this file,
owned by `nijil-deriv`.

## What is tracked instead

Delivery is tracked as specs and changes rather than as a goals document:
shipped behaviour in [`openspec/specs/`](../openspec/specs/), in-flight work in
[`openspec/changes/`](../openspec/changes/).

Quality measures the repo does enforce, as committed baselines rather than
targets, live in [`baselines/`](baselines/): bundle size, chunk names, route
map, alias usage, and knip dead-code counts.
